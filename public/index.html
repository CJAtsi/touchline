Fixture difficulty formula

Affects the FDR pills shown next to fixtures elsewhere on the site (Player stats detail panel, squad builder) — not the club rankings list below, which has its own weights.

Formula: blend of the opponent's attack / defence / overall strength (their away ratings when you're at home, home ratings when you're away), plus a small home-advantage adjustment. Attack weight = how much the opponent's attacking threat counts against you; defence weight = how much their defensive solidity counts; overall weight = their general quality on top of that; home advantage = a fixed discount applied when you're playing at home. All weights are yours to change — this recomputes every fixture pill on the site immediately.
Club rankings

This list (below) is controlled by these weights, not the fixture-difficulty ones above. Squad weight = how much this season's actual squad (points once underway, price before that) counts; strength weight = how much FPL's longer-run club strength rating counts.
