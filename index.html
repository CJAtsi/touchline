From to

Click any fixture pill for a full scoreline estimate, or click the Avg FDR / Avg GF / Avg GA column headers to sort by them directly. The current gameweek's column is highlighted. Scorelines use the same Weibull-count + copula model (win/draw/loss blended with Pi-ratings and real odds where available — see "Edit prediction model" on the Rankings tab).

Fixture swing — prime wildcard / free-hit windows

Scans the whole rest of the season for gameweeks where several teams' fixtures swing from hard to easy at the same time — that clustering is the real signal for when to wildcard or free-hit, since it means a big chunk of the player pool opens up together rather than just one team having a good run.

Chip advisor — Bench Boost / Triple Captain timing

Candidate gameweeks for your two single-gameweek chips. Bench Boost wants every squad player (especially the bench) to have a fixture, ideally with a double gameweek somewhere in the 15; Triple Captain wants your single best captaincy option (reusing the same scoring as the Captaincy tab) to have an exceptional fixture. If you've loaded a squad on the My team tab it's used directly — otherwise this falls back to a pool-wide signal. Heuristic ranking, not a guarantee — treat it as a shortlist to sanity-check yourself, not an auto-pick.

Best fixture pairings

Pairs of clubs whose fixtures rotate well together over the same From/To range above — each gameweek, whichever of the two has the easier fixture is the one you'd use, so a pairing scores well even if neither club is easy every single week on its own. Change the mode pill near the top of the page (FDR / pGoals / Win% / Clean sheet % / etc.) to filter this table for a specific kind of return.

Fixture run finder

Finds each club's best run of favourable fixtures in the range above, across three tiers. A run doesn't need every single game to be easy — it just needs the average FDR across the run to stay under the tier's threshold, so one tougher game can be absorbed if the rest of the run is easy enough. Fun facts

Insights

Differentials, price-change signals, squad news, mini-league rival comparison, and projection accuracy tracking — all built from real FPL API fields already loaded elsewhere in the app. Heuristic panels are clearly labelled as such.

Differentials — low ownership, high upside

Players under your ownership ceiling, ranked by projected points (Touchline's own next-GW model, same as Player Stats' "Next GW proj."). Simple, explainable ranking — filtered by ownership, then sorted by projected points, no hidden scoring formula.

Price watch — likely risers & fallers

Heuristic estimate, not a guarantee — there's no official/reliable external price-prediction feed, so this uses a well-known proxy real FPL price-tracking sites rely on: net transfer momentum THIS gameweek relative to the player's own ownership base (see priceChangeSignal() in code for the exact formula/thresholds). It can miss FPL's actual overnight price-change algorithm, which factors in more than is exposed via the public API.

Squad news — injuries, doubts, suspensions

Every player who isn't 100% fully fit, straight from the FPL API's own status/news/chance_of_playing fields — real live data, not an estimate. Shortlisted players are pinned to the top, then sorted by chance of playing (lowest/most concerning first).

Rivals — mini-league & head-to-head team comparison

Enter a rival manager's Team ID (their FPL "entry" ID — visible in the URL of their Points page, e.g. .../entry/1234567/event/5) to compare their most recent gameweek picks against your own squad (loaded on the My team tab). Uses the same proxied /entry/{id}/... endpoints as My team, so it works with zero extra setup.

Projection accuracy tracker

Snapshots the whole player pool's pre-deadline projected points each gameweek (stored locally in your browser), then once that gameweek finishes, compares them against real points scored. Known limitation: this app doesn't have historical snapshots of what it would have projected in past gameweeks before today, so tracking is forward-looking only — it starts building a record from whichever gameweek is current when you first open this panel, and grows a longer track record the more gameweeks you play through with the app open.

Rankings

Two separate, independent systems live on this page — they don't affect each other. Fixture difficulty (below) colours individual fixtures. Club rankings (further down) is a single best-to-worst list of all 20 clubs.

1. Fixture difficulty formula

Controls the colour-coded FDR pills shown next to fixtures everywhere on the site (this tab's fixture list, Player stats detail panel, squad builder, the FDR tab). Each slider is how much one trait of your opponent counts against you when judging how hard a fixture is — higher = that trait matters more.

Try it: two example fixtures
Prediction model

Powers the predicted scorelines and win/draw/loss % on the FDR tab. Win/draw/loss % blends three sources: Pi-ratings (an iterative team-strength rating built from every real result this season — not a static preseason number), the scoreline model itself, and real betting odds when available (needs the Worker's /odds route + a free API key — see the deploy notes). The exact predicted score comes from a Weibull-count distribution with a copula linking the two teams' goal counts, replacing the previous Poisson/Dixon-Coles model.

Saved setting slots

Save the current FDR weights, club ranking weights, and prediction-model weights together as a named slot, so you can switch between setups without re-entering every value. Team overrides and fixture-run tiers aren't included — those persist on their own regardless of which slot is active.

Export my settings

Downloads everything Touchline has saved in this browser — player attribute edits, prior-league projections, club overrides, main positions, shortlist, and all the formula weights above — as one JSON file. Use this to back up your setup, move it to another browser, or send it to whoever maintains the site to set as the default a first-time visitor sees.

2. Club rankings

A single ranked list of all 20 clubs (below), best to worst — separate from the fixture-difficulty formula above and not used by it. Each slider is how much one signal counts toward a club's overall rank.

Edit club strength

Disagree with FPL's rating for a club? Set Attack, Defence and Overall individually — 1 (weakest) to 5 (strongest). These feed both the FDR formula above (opponent pills, club rankings) and the predicted scoreline on the FDR fixture modal — change a club's Attack here and their predicted scorelines update to match. Starts at FPL's own computed rating for each field; hit ↺ Reset to go back to all three.

Quick calibration questionnaire

A faster way to tune club strength than dragging every slider above — answer a continuous stream of "does X have a better attack/defence than Y" questions, and each answer nudges those two clubs' Attack/Defence override up or down slightly. Keep going as long as you like; every answer compounds on the last, so your gut feel converges toward a full set of ratings over time. Skip any question you're unsure about.
